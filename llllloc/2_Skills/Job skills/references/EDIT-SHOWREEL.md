## THE RECIPE
1. Length 45 to 60 s (median of the 16: 58 s). Over 70 s only with a decade of film behind it.
2. The name rides on a piece of work inside 2 s (name on a tennis ball, four words on four ribbons). A plain logo card over 5 s reads slow; four of the sixteen do it and all four feel it.
3. The first work shot lands on the drop of the track (THE LINE 3.0 s, Art&Graft 6.4 s, Yatta 1.7 s).
4. The densest 10 s of cutting sits inside the first 20 s (13 of 16 reels in the first half; median 10 cuts, max 17). The montage is the hook, not the climax.
5. Project runs of 2 to 4 shots, then a jump. One shot per project reads as a feed, not a reel.
6. A wave of pace: 10 to 14 cuts per 10 s in the peak, 4 to 6 in the slow bins. ASL median 1.42 s, MSL 1.02 s, ratio 0.81. Bursts of 3 to 6 shots at 4 to 9 frames inside the peak; one or two holds of 3 to 6 s for a hero shot.
7. Transitions: hard cuts over 90 percent, a few white or colour flash cuts, field swaps (colour ground changes, subject carries over). Dissolves: none confirmed in 16 reels.
8. End card 3 s, name plus site. Over 5 s is the first note reviewers give.
9. One track, 100 to 150 bpm, with a verse and a chorus so the drop exists. Cut on hits and phrases, not every beat: half the reels cut on the beat above chance, the rest cut on picture and feel no less musical.
10. Master to the platform. The batch sits at minus 8 to minus 13 LUFS with true peaks over 0 dBTP; web norm is minus 14 LUFS, minus 1 dBTP.

## WHAT MAKES IT LAND
- Name on the work (INFRAME, Clim): the viewer never waits. Without it, 6 to 8 s of card before the first shot.
- The early peak (Art&Graft 17 cuts in 13 to 23 s): the promise is made while the viewer is still deciding. Without it, the reel builds to a climax nobody waits for.
- Project runs (Yatta 3 to 4 shots per client): each client gets a mini story, the reel reads as work, not a mood board. Without it (AS reel, 64 projects in 60 s) it reads as a feed.
- The wave (every reel but Yatta and darkpulse): fast bins against slow bins; a flat 10 per 10 s for 55 s (darkpulse) tires by 40 s.
- A hold inside the speed (THE LINE 5.4 s, INFRAME 6.3 s, Giovane 6 s): the one shot that proves craft, placed where the track breathes.

## WHAT THE RESEARCH FOUND

Ben Marriott, 20 Tips For An Amazing Showreel (YouTube 2020, 213k views): hirers decide in 20 s, front-load, faster percussive track to cut on. r/MotionDesign 2025 to 2026 (128 to 174 upvotes): name intro super short, drop label tiles, end card too long is the first note, most reviewers watch muted, competing motion inside a quick cut loses the eye. Beat-sync guides: cut on phrases of 2, 4, 8 beats, biggest visual on the drop; no primary study found. THE LINE 2025: bespoke music by Rina May Project (YouTube description, 30 May 2025).
