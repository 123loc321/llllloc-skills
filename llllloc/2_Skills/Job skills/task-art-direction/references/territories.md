# TASK: TERRITORIES

JOB: give him three concept directions that differ in the idea, so he chooses an idea, not a color.

INPUT: `*_CLAUDE/4_Story/BRIEF.md`. `BRAND-<Client>.md` if it exists. His references or board, if the job has them.

STEPS
1. Answer four questions for THIS brief, in writing, for yourself:
   a. What does the subject's own world look like (its materials, places, objects)?
   b. What does the target feeling look like as a frame (longing = empty space, urgency = compression, awe = one thing too big for the frame)?
   c. What does the screen demand (a phone feed fights for its first second, an event screen is watched from far)?
   d. What does every other film on this subject look like? That is THE DEFAULT. Write it down in one sentence.
2. Draft five ideas, one from each path: the subject's world, the feeling, the audience (meet or break their expectation), THE DEFAULT turned inside out, an unusual format (a letter, a countdown, a map, a recipe, a front page).
3. For each draft, estimate how likely a model given this brief would produce it. At least two of the five must be below 10 percent. If not, draft again. These numbers stay private.
4. Sketch each idea as rough blocks in a frame. Two ideas with the same block layout are one idea. Replace one.
5. Keep three. At least two of the three must be from the below-10-percent group.
6. Write each territory in the shape below.
7. Recommend one, with one sentence of reason. Write the recommendation AFTER all three, never first.

OUTPUT: `*_CLAUDE/4_Story/TERRITORIES_V<n>.md`. Each territory:
- NAME: two or three words that describe the direction (never "Option A").
- THE IDEA: one sentence with a clear mechanism.
- THE PICTURE: the opening frame in concrete terms (subject, lens, light, material, color roles). One reference image from his board or a named film or photographer, with what to take from it and what NOT to take.
- THE MOVE: how it moves and sounds (rhythm, camera, transition, sonic motif).
- THE RISK: the most likely reason it fails, and how to handle it.
- THE COST: what it needs to make (AI shots, Cavalry, shoot, edit days).
Then THE DEFAULT (the generic version, named so he sees what was avoided), then the recommendation.
The chat gets the file card plus one line: the three names.

RULES
- R1. Different means a different idea or story, not a different palette. Why: three colorways of one idea waste his choice. Check: do the three IDEA lines describe three different mechanisms?
- R2. A direction is a picture, never an adjective (his rule, 10 Sep 2026). Why: "premium" means nothing to a model or a client. Check: does each PICTURE line name a lens, a light or a material?
- R3. Stop after the three. Why: he picks first. Check: no treatment, deck or prompt was started.

CHECK: three territories, six parts each, THE DEFAULT named, recommendation last.
