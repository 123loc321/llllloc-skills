# TASK: TREATMENT

JOB: write the picked territory as a director treatment a client can read and approve.

INPUT: the picked territory (name it), `*_CLAUDE/4_Story/BRIEF.md`, `BRAND-<Client>.md` if it exists, the VO script if one exists.

STEPS
1. Write the sections below in order. Skip a section the job does not need, and say so in one line at the end.
2. Each section does one job. If two sections say the same thing, cut one.
3. Tie every look and sound choice to what the audience must feel or understand.
4. Read it once as the client. Cut every sentence that only sounds good.

OUTPUT: `*_CLAUDE/4_Story/TREATMENT_V<n>.md`, sections in this order:
1. OPENING: the feeling the film leaves, in two or three sentences.
2. THE BRIEF, AS WE READ IT: the problem and the chance, in plain words.
3. THE IDEA: the one-sentence mechanism.
4. THE STORY: beginning, turn, end. Beats with rough timing.
5. THE WORLD: place, light, color roles, materials, texture.
6. CAMERA: lens family, height, distance, movement, and when it breaks its own rule.
7. MOTION AND EDIT: rhythm, transitions, what carries across each cut.
8. SOUND: music feeling, sound motif, where silence is used.
9. THE BRAND: how the product and logo enter without stopping the story.
10. FORMATS: the cutdowns, ratios and stills it becomes.
11. HOW WE MAKE IT: AI generation, Cavalry, shoot, edit. Honest about risk.
12. CLOSE: why this is the right film, in two sentences.
The chat gets the reveal card plus one line.

RULES
- R1. Written in his voice: short, sure, concrete. Why: the client reads it as the studio speaking. Check: no sentence over 25 words, no stacked adjectives.
- R2. VO lines, client copy and names stay word for word. Why: they are the client's content. Check: each quoted line matches its source.
- R3. Never promise what the tools cannot make. Why: a treatment is a promise. Check: every shot in THE STORY is listed as makeable in HOW WE MAKE IT.

CHECK: twelve sections or a named skip, no repeated idea across sections, every look choice tied to a reason.
