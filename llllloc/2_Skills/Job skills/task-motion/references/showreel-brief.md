## THE SHOWREEL BRIEF [R27] [R28] [R31]
Four lines on top of the `## Motion` section of `DesignSystem_<Name>_V<N>.md` (older project: `LOOK-LOCK.md`; no folder: on top of the build script), written by the lead before V1. A piece past V1 fills them from its design system as it stands. They serve the design system, the brand and the VO, never the reverse. A brief he pastes replaces them.
1. THE CLASS. The best piece of its kind, and 2 or 3 real ones from THE GRADE. Aim past them.
2. WHAT IT IS NOT. The defaults of this genre, named and refused.
3. THE LANGUAGE. Three to five physical qualities of the motion, found for this piece in its brief, brand, VO and references. Never taken from a list, an example or a past film.
4. THE SIGNATURE. The one move a viewer would replay, and the VO word it lands on.

THE TEST, every version, on the contact sheet and in the live preview: would each frame make a top studio's reel? Fix it, or name it as the least-sure choice. The V1 reply shows the four lines, so he can steer. Lanes never rewrite them (RULE 8).
