Task Builder

Fill this in, then hit EXPORT — it saves the brief and all your files into one folder you choose. Drag that whole folder into the Claude chat and it reads the brief, maps every file, and writes the prompt. Set the mode, sort your files into what goes into the AI vs what's just context, and describe the task.

02Modelpick a mode first

Pick one or more. With several selected, the AI researches each and writes one prompt built to work across all of them.

Video mode
7–12 cuts inside ONE ~15-second clip. Busy, fast-cutting, self-contained — the whole idea in a single generation.
Shotlist mode
Splits the film into sets of 1–3 cuts per 15-second prompt. Slower, more room per shot, many prompts strung across the whole story.
English only
One clean English prompt. The default — research still reads Chinese + English sources.
Chinese + English
Chinese-first (often stronger on Seedance) plus a faithful English version to review.
